This is a valueless testing build. Winiwa and xWiniwa are test assets with no monetary value. It
is not a stablecoin launch, trading release or production-readiness claim.

## What is new since v0.0.11.39

- Your bank tells you when it is out of date. A phone that has been offline too long to catch up
  on its own now says so on the Wallet page, in place of the balance, and offers one action:
  Resync with the network. The message also arrives once as a pop-up. A first run is never told
  this. Settings, Network reads how far behind the bank is and whether the gap is closing.
- Nothing is called failed while your node still has it. A faucet claim the node had accepted was
  marked failed after three minutes while the same node was relaying it. A claim the node can see
  now waits, and a claim that genuinely fails hands the faucet countdown back.
- Payment progress shows the time each step finished and how long the current step has run.
  Broadcasting says when the bank has no peers to relay to, instead of spinning; the Settings
  network line says Alone or how many nodes it is connected to.
- Fingerprint or face is offered in the open. Under Payment protection, "How you confirm a
  protected send" has two rows: Payment code (Set, then Change) and Fingerprint or face. A row the
  phone cannot honour is greyed out and says why.
- Commas for thousands everywhere, inputs included: 1,000 reads as 1,000 in every figure and every
  amount field.
- Opening the app asks the node once for what several parts of the app want at the same moment
  (255 node commands at open became 177), and the resync no longer shows a connect-a-node flash.
- Mint and burn work on a fresh node: the vault's coins were readable all along, the app was
  asking the node the wrong question.
- StablesAgent answers on the first try. Receive keeps its dark colour; Send sits on the right,
  Receive on the left, everywhere the two are offered together. Recent activity on the Wallet page
  has See all. The camera's Close button is replaced by the restart button when the camera is off.
  The network contribution charts sit under their own measures with labelled axes.

Install over the previous version. Do not uninstall: the wallet lives on the device.
