# Stables v0.0.11.60 testing prerelease

This is a valueless testing build. Winiwa and xWiniwa are test assets with no monetary value. It
is not a stablecoin launch, trading release or production-readiness claim.

## What is released

- `Stables_v0.0.11.60.apk`: the standalone Android app with an embedded Minima node. Package
  `org.stablescouncil.stables`, versionCode 11060. Install over the previous version. Do not
  uninstall: the wallet lives on the device.
  SHA-256: `f6d20a7afdb444d479abd1978b04175b7e072fa53dd0ece142f21dd87a75faff`
- `StablesCore_v0.0.11.60.apk`: the Minima Core companion, released for the first time. It has no node
  of its own and no Internet permission: it pairs with the official Minima Core Android app on the
  same phone and uses that node and that wallet. Package `org.stablescouncil.stables.core`,
  versionCode 11060. Requires Minima Core (tested with 1.6.11). Test tokens only, no value.
  SHA-256: `266d017709fb4c16f9d4461d059102fdecbf731304186e4712564fa320063640`
- Both APKs are signed with the same certificate, SHA-256 `dabb1b2a79b134b6008e6401735d649c140b51f2c4a83eb001b2ffdad5ce5dd4`.

The companion is published as pairing-tested, not rehearsed: on one phone it reconnected to Minima
Core after install, after a Core restart and after its own restart, claimed from the faucet, and
minted, partially burned and fully burned xWiniwa through Core, each proven on the chain. The
three-wallet rehearsal on independent devices has not happened yet. A send through Core needs the
payment code set in the app; that step was left to the wallet owner.

## What is new since v0.0.11.53

- A second mint or burn waits for the first to land. Every mint and burn spends the vault's one
  balance state coin, so two in flight are a double spend and the network keeps only the first.
  The app used to guard that with a 70 second timer; measured on a phone, a burn took four minutes
  to mine and a second one started three minutes later vanished in silence. The guard now holds
  until the coin the operation spent is gone from the node, survives a restart, and lets the next
  operation through the moment the chain has moved. Meanwhile a second attempt gets one sentence:
  your previous mint or burn is still being confirmed.
- A mint or burn the network dropped is marked failed within minutes, with the balance corrected,
  and it stays failed across restarts.
- A settling claim or burn is credited once, never twice: the Winiwa row and the total now agree
  while a transaction is landing.
- A payment that would spend too many notes combines them first, then continues. Pre-approved by
  default through a new Wallet management setting, Combine notes when a payment needs it; turned
  off, the app asks first with the exact figures.
- Your bank tells you when it has drifted off the network and offers the fix. A node that has
  come back on its own divergent copy of history can look connected and moving while sharing
  nothing with everyone else. The app now reads this from the chain itself and shows one message,
  Your bank is out of sync, with the resync as the action; on the companion it opens Minima Core.
  Nothing is shown while the bank is healthy.
- The burn confirmation wears the same red as the burn button.
- StablesAgent is told what you are looking at and what was just said, and answers once.
