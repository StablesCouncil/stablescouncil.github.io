# About Stables: the presentation page

*Source: https://stablescouncil.org/about.html. Written 2026-11-08. This is what the page says, for answering questions about it. Stables is in its testing phase: when someone asks what they can do today, `release_scope_boundary.md` decides.*

The homepage's "Want to know more?" opens this page. Send people there for the general picture.

## The objective

A banking system that belongs to the people and merchants who use it, built to keep running forever. Everybody is their own bank, and together we are the network.

## For merchants, by merchants

Stables is built around merchants, because money is useful only where it can be spent.

**For a merchant's own business (the design):**

- **Open an account in minutes.** Install the app on a phone and the account exists. No forms, no file to submit, no bank to convince.
- **No authorisation needed.** The merchant decides and is in. The account is theirs for as long as they want it.
- **Paid in seconds.** Holding two phones together took about 2 seconds in our tests, including with no network. A payment sent over the Minima network took about half a minute. These are measurements with test tokens on our own phones, not a guarantee.
- **Keep more of every sale.** Each payment goes straight from the customer to the merchant, and a phone is all the equipment needed.
- **Usual prices.** A merchant prices in the currency its customers know: 1 USDs for 1 dollar's worth of goods.
- **Their money, their customers.** Takings stay under the merchant's own keys, and customer relationships stay theirs.

**Together, owning the infrastructure (the design):** the merchants and people who use Stables are its infrastructure, and they own it together.

- Each phone running Stables can run a Minima node and help carry the network.
- The Stables Council, the community, holds the system and decides who operates each role, the terms and the fees, with roles awarded by bidding and pay that rises with quality. The development team operates every role today, and each passes step by step to operators the Council chooses. Who votes is the Council's first decision.
- Fees and listing fees go to shared funds that serve the system and pay for its growth, as voted by the community.
- Anyone can take a stake in the system's equity through xMinima. It rises and falls with MINIMA and can lose value. Never present it as an investment opportunity or promise a return.

**Support: the ambassadors (designed, not available in the test).** An ambassador is a local professional, often a merchant too, who sets a merchant up and stays their contact, and is paid to see the business grow. Accepting payments is free. Listing in the merchant directory is a choice, and costs the price of 15 Big Mac® for a year with an ambassador or 16 without. Details: https://stablescouncil.org/ambassadorsprogramdesc.html

## For their customers (the design)

Ready in minutes with no bank account and nobody's approval. Keys held on the person's own phone; Stables never asks for a seed phrase. Pay by touch, even offline. A payment carries an address, not a name. The app shows a balance as ready only when it can prove the money is there. Holding your own money is a responsibility: nobody can reset access, so the seed phrase must be kept written down and private.

## Decentralisation and Minima

Decentralisation means no single person, company or server is in charge: no head office to call and no central switch to turn off. It makes possible access nobody can revoke, payments with no borders in the way, rules anyone can check, and a system that outlives its builders. Where we stand: the Stables lab still holds the test keys of the card's approvers and guardians, which is acceptable only because the tokens have no value. Before any real money, those keys must be spread across independent operators.

Why Minima: every user can run a full node on their own device, including a phone, and every user helps build and validate the chain. A Minima script can hold money with no owner key.

## Values

Secure, Pseudonymous, Unstoppable, Free choice, Open, Honest. The rule for every part: no single part can steal the funds or lock them for good, and if one part stops, the rest still pays.

## Roadmap

No stage has a date. The short version, from the Navigation System (https://stablescouncil.org/council_navigation_system.html):

- **Done:** showcase and demo.
- **Live in test:** one Android app with its own Minima node, wallet and activity, send and receive by QR, on-chain test tokens, security settings, feedback and the Academy.
- **In progress:** the Stables card and its guardians, independent key holders, full wallet recovery on a second device, the desktop app, and the card, Minima Bay and the Bridge strengthened as one structure.
- **Merchants:** point of sale, invoices and QR, shop directory, on and off ramps and a merchant API are being drafted; the ambassador network prepares merchants, then the first local clusters.
- **The money:** mint and burn rules, peg mechanics, Coverage Funds, liquidity and a public balance sheet.
- **The Council:** creation and charter, seats and voting design, then each role handed, step by step, to operators the Council chooses.
- **Then:** an external audit of the scripts and the economics, once everything learned in testing has been folded in.
- **Later:** production. The Stables Council decides when real money is allowed, and a public subscription in Minima, for the stablecoin and for xMinima, creates the first stablecoin supply, with no key on the vault.
- **Where it leads:** a sovereign banking device, a financial system builders can rely on with fees shared back to the community, and an autonomous Council with pseudonymous identity and self-growing local clusters.

## Answering rules for this topic

- Say what is design and what exists. Stablecoins, merchant tools, the directory, the Ambassador program, Coverage Funds and trading are not available today.
- The speed figures are test measurements. Do not promise them.
- The Stables card is an experiment and needs a security review by independent experts before production use.
- Never promise yield, returns or a price.
- Keep the message positive: say what Stables brings. Do not compare it with banks or anyone else, and do not criticise them.
