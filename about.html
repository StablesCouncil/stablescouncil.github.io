Be your bank.

Stables is a payment infrastructure made for merchants and their customers. Open an account in minutes, no authorisation needed, and get paid in seconds.

Our objective

A banking system that belongs to the people and merchants who use it, built to keep running forever.

We bring everyday money that anyone can use freely, in the currencies people already think in. Everybody is their own bank, and together we are the network.

For merchants, by merchants

Money is useful only where it can be spent. Independent merchants make that true, one shop at a time, so the system is built around them.

Together: owning the infrastructure

The merchants and the people who use Stables are its infrastructure, and they own it together.

Before you start

Holding your own money is also a responsibility.

You alone hold your access, so keep your seed phrase written down and private. The tokens in this test have no value, and nothing here is an offer of returns.

Decentralisation

The network belongs to everyone who takes part.

In a decentralised network the rules are public, and the participants apply them together. Each of them can check for themselves that the rules were followed.

What it makes possible

Access that stays yours

Your savings sit at your own address under your own keys, for as long as you want.

One network, worldwide

Paying someone abroad works the same way as paying someone next door.

Rules anyone can check

Anyone can read the scripts and see on chain exactly what happened.

A system that outlives its builders

Our goal is that people can always reach their money, whatever happens to any server or team. We test it: a phone answered another phone's request for proofs while the approvers' server was stopped.

Where we stand

Independent key holders come next.

The development team operates every role today, while the tokens have no value. Before any real money, the keys go to independent operators, and over the coming months and years each role passes to operators chosen by the Council.

Why Minima

Decentralisation is for everyone, and Minima makes that real. On Minima, every user can run a full node on their own device, including a phone.

A full node on your phone

The Stables app runs its own Minima node, or uses MinimaCore on the same phone. Your phone reads the chain itself and knows the truth first hand.

Users build the chain

Every user helps build and validate the chain, so the network is carried by the people who use it.

Money held by rules

A Minima script can hold money with no owner key. The vault behind the Stables card pays only by its own rules.

How we intend to do it

1

Currencies on Minima

Stables are currencies you hold yourself, such as USDs and EURs. They are minted by locking MINIMA and burned to get MINIMA back.

2

Merchants as the anchor

Merchants accept Stables at one for one for goods, and can swap cash for Stables locally. Stability comes from that everyday acceptance and the incentives around it.

3

Risk taken knowingly

People who choose xMinima take on the price swings of MINIMA, and a Coverage Fund adds a second buffer. Both carry a real risk of loss.

4

Rules in scripts

The rules live in scripts on Minima, and guardians can stop a wrong payout. The Stables Council will hold and maintain the whole structure.

Today: you can claim the test tokens Winiwa and xWiniwa on Minima mainnet, mint, burn, send and receive them, and try the Stables card. Stablecoins, trading and Coverage Funds are being built.

Our roadmap

Each stage opens once the one before it has proven itself. This is the short version of our Navigation System, where every item shows its status and progress.

  1. Done

    Showcase and demo

    The first previews, then live sends and QR payments on Minima.

  2. Live in test

    The foundations

    One Android app with its own Minima node. Wallet and activity, send and receive by QR, on-chain test tokens, security settings, feedback and the Academy.

  3. In progress

    The card and its safety

    The Stables card and its guardians, independent key holders, full wallet recovery on a second device, and the desktop app. The card, Minima Bay and the Bridge are strengthened as one structure.

  4. Merchants

    The tools and the people

    Point of sale, invoices and QR, shop directory, on and off ramps, and a merchant API are being drafted. The ambassador network prepares merchants, then the first local clusters.

  5. The money

    Currencies and their stability

    Mint and burn rules, peg mechanics, Coverage Funds, liquidity and a public balance sheet, each checked before it is claimed.

  6. The Council

    Handing over to the community

    Council creation and charter, seats and voting design, then each role handed, step by step, to operators the Council chooses.

  7. Then

    External audit

    An independent review of the scripts and the economics, once everything learned in testing has been folded in.

  8. Later

    Production

    The Stables Council decides when real money is allowed. A single Council genesis mint creates the first stablecoin supply.
