Card Guardians

Test tokens, lab keys Vault R2T5

The money behind every Stables card sits in a vault on Minima. Guardians are independent programs, each run by a different person with their own key, that watch that vault day and night. Any one guardian can stop a payout it finds wrong. Guardians can never pay anyone.

When everything works
  • Paying needs no one. Card money moves from phone to phone by touch, offline, with no server.
  • Your money is back in Savings within hours. You ask from the app, the approvers check and approve, and after a 2-hour safety wait the vault pays. Recent requests took about 2 to 4 hours in all.
  • Nobody holds the money. The vault is a script with no key. It pays only by its own rules, so neither the approvers, nor the guardians, nor the Stables lab can take money out.
What keeps it safe and unstoppable
  • Every payout is checked twice. First by the approvers, then by the nine guardians during the 2-hour wait. One guardian stops a wrong payout; three freeze everything.
  • If the approvers do not answer, the vault still lets your money come back. After 3 days, a request they neither approve nor refuse can be paid with no approver key. The guardian program that will post that payout by itself is being built; until then it is posted by hand.
  • If the approvers disappear, nobody can steal through their absence. Payouts pause instead of opening. After 3 days of silence, six of the nine guardians install new approvers, and payouts resume.
  • Known limit, fix designed: if the approvers explicitly refuse a valid request, that money stays locked in the vault. Until the fix, the Stables lab pays it back by hand. The fix lets five guardians overrule a refusal.

All of this is written in the vault's script on Minima, not in a server.

Today every seat holds a key kept by the Stables lab, and the tokens have no value. The first independent guardians will run on a phone, a personal computer and a separate server, none of them the approvers' server.

Who does what
The vault
A script on Minima, no key

Holds: the money behind every card.

Pays only: an approved request once its wait is over, or a request left unanswered for 3 days.

Nobody can change it or take money out any other way.

See the vault on the Minima explorer
The approvers
11 keys, 7 must sign

Do: approve or refuse each request to move card money back to Savings, and confirm every 6 hours that they are alive.

Cannot: pay before the wait, lift a freeze, or choose the guardians.

Today: one server, held by the Stables lab.

The approvers' program on GitHub (test version 0.1)
The guardians
9 seats

Do: re-check every request and every approval. 1 stops a payout, 3 freeze all, 5 lift a freeze, 6 replace silent approvers.

Cannot: pay anyone or approve anything.

Today: every seat held by the Stables lab.
